# CURRICULUM — the mātṛkā install, ten stages

> Human-readable mirror of `data/curriculum.json` (source of truth, test-enforced).
> ASSEMBLY: order, weeks, counts and bars are modern apparatus; every practice element
> traces to MV / TĀ 15 / PTv / VBT (attested column). Rules: one tradition per sitting ·
> natural breath always (TĀ 4.91) · stop always available · gates are apparatus, the real
> criterion is anusamdhāna (identity-attention), not counts.

## The arc

```text
stages 1–4   INSTALL      sound + touch + locus → recall without the chart
stages 5–6   REVERSE      withdrawal direction + rapid reinstall
stages 7–8   USE          the installed body moves as mantra
stages 9–10  REWRITE + WITHDRAW   Mālinī second sky (gated) · gaze turns inward
```

## The ten stages

| # | Stage | Nightly | Where (lab) | Gate | Attested |

**Timing:** weeks are guidance, gates are law — don't advance because the calendar says
so. Months 3–4 earliest for stage 9, only after Mātṛkā is automatic.
