# Phase 1 — Chant phonemes at the body

> **This is the start of the pathway.** Do this until Mātṛkā install feels like a field, not a list.
> Protocol JSON: `protocols/phase1_chant_body.json` · Theory: `docs/PATHWAY.md`

---

## Frame (say once, then drop it)

Nyāsa is not "shoulder emits ka." Nyāsa is:

> reconstructing this biological body as the body of Śakti by installing the matrix from which articulated experience arises.

Arc: **ordinary → mapped → mantra-body → deity-body → cosmos-body.**

Flood: the body is **constructed through inscription**, not discovered as anatomy.

---

## Setup

- Seat spine long, jaw soft
- Audio models optional: `/root/sanskrithelp/public/audio/phonemes/*.ogg`
- Breath: **natural** — chant may lengthen exhale. No forced 1:4:2.
- Primary visual object: **Devanāgarī glyph**
- Touch lightly with right hand

---

## Circuit (~30 min)

### 0. Seat + frame (1 min)

Intention only. Drop theory after.

### 1. Breath + voice (2 min)

5–10 easy breaths. Speech will ride prāṇa.

### 2. Vowels on head/face (8 min)

For each: **see glyph → touch locus → chant once → feel location.**

| Sound | Glyph | Locus | PTv secondary |
|-------|-------|-------|---------------|
| a | अ | forehead | anuttara / cit |
| ā | आ | mouth/face | ānanda |
| i | इ | right eye | icchā |
| ī | ई | left eye | icchā |
| u | उ | right ear | jñāna |
| ū | ऊ | left ear | jñāna |
| ṛ | ऋ | right nostril | — |
| ṝ | ॠ | left nostril | — |
| ḷ | ऌ | right cheek | — |
| ḹ | ॡ | left cheek | — |
| e | ए | lower teeth | kriyā |
| ai | ऐ | upper teeth | kriyā |
| o | ओ | lower lip | kriyā |
| au | औ | upper lip | kriyā |
| aṃ | अं | crown / śikhā | bindu mark |
| aḥ | अः | tongue | visarga |

**Short night:** a · ā · i · ī · aṃ · aḥ only.

PTv power names are **secondary** — glyph + sound + locus first.

### 3. ka-varga — right upper limb (4 min)

```text
ka  → right shoulder    # velar, plain stop
kha → right arm         # + wind
ga  → right elbow       # + hum/glow
gha → right wrist       # hum + wind
ṅa  → fingers right     # nasal tunnel
```

Chant each once while touching. Then chant the five as **one continuous arc**.

### 4. ca-varga — left upper limb (4 min)

```text
ca cha ja jha ña
left shoulder → arm → elbow → wrist → fingers
```

Palate place. Same manner ladder. Then right arc + left arc on natural breath.

### 5. Lower limbs (4 min · optional short)

```text
ṭa ṭha ḍa ḍha ṇa   right lower limb
ta tha da dha na    left lower limb
```

If short on time: one varga only.

### 6. Torso + deep (3 min · shorten freely)

```text
pa pha ba bha ma    sides → spine → belly → heart
ya ra la va         skin → blood → flesh → sinews
śa ṣa sa ha kṣa     bone → marrow → essence → prāṇa → generative
```

Minimum viable: **pa pha ba bha ma** then **ha kṣa**. Longer pause at **ma / heart**.

### 7. Totalization pass (5 min)

One continuous chant: **full vowels then full consonants a→kṣa.**

Touch optional. Goal: body feels **filled as a field**. End in silence at crown (aṃ) / tongue (aḥ).

### 8. aham contraction (3 min)

Stop chanting. Natural breath.

```text
a  (forehead / emergence)
ha (terminal edge / prāṇa)
ṃ  (bindu / crown)
```

Let a…kṣa contract toward **aham**. Rest in reflexive I. If it rises, let a…kṣa expand once.

Master rhythm — not occult collecting. All fifty differentiations arise in the field of aham.

### 9. Silence (2 min)

No manipulation.

One question, felt not answered: **are these locations mechanisms outside me, or expressions of the awareness installing them?**

### 10. Log

`phase1 | installed | easiest locus | totalization? y/n | tension?`

## Progression signal

You're ready for Phase 2 when:

- You can chant a varga and feel its limb arc without looking
- Vowels on head/face fire glyph+locus together
- Totalization pass feels like one body-field, not 50 stickers
- aham contraction is available without strain

Phase 2 = dual-coordinate objects + random locate drill (`data/phoneme_objects.json`, `data/articulation_matrix.json`).

## What NOT to do in Phase 1

| Avoid | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Invent physiology for each locus | Texts often don't give it |
| Force retention / 1:4:2 | TĀ 4.91 — don't torment the body |
| Chase Mālinī first | Master Mātṛkā order first |
| One universal chakra chart | Multilayered overlays only |
| Collect deities like Pokémon | Pratyabhijñā = recognition |
| Mix other traditions' rites | Content is tradition-specific |
