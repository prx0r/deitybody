# Daily circuit — Bruno × Abhinavagupta

**OS:** Abhinavagupta/Trika · **Machinery:** Bruno wheels · **Repo:** deitybody

Duration: 30–45 min. Do this until the five consonant families are felt categories.

---

## 1. Articulation family (5–10 min)

One varga only. Exaggerate every feature in the mouth:

```text
ka kha ga gha ṅa     # cave · plain stop → wind → glow → glow+wind → tunnel
```

Then reduce exaggeration until pronunciation remains exact.

Ask at each sound: **where is this physically made?**

Audio models: `/root/sanskrithelp/public/audio/phonemes/*.ogg`

---

## 2. Glyph + sound (10 min)

Speak each phoneme while seeing its Devanāgarī.

Eyes closed → generate glyph + sound together.

Target: घ fires velar+voiced+aspirated+glyph together.

---

## 3. Dual-coordinate nyāsa (10 min)

Install same sounds in **tantric loci** (from `data/phoneme_objects.json`).

1. With touch (right hand indicates locus)
2. Without touch (internal only)

Primary visual object = the glyph. Colors optional/pedagogical.

---

## 4. Random locate (5 min)

Hear a phoneme (or say it yourself randomly).

Locate instantly:

1. mouth production locus
2. tantric locus

Both must fire. If only one does, that coordinate is weak.

---

## 5. Full sequence + internal (5 min)

Chant complete current sequence once.

Reproduce internally without moving the mouth.

Log one line: `family | easiest dual-coord | any mismatch mouth vs tantric`.
