# Month 1 card — become the vowels

**OS:** Abhinavagupta/Trika · **Repo:** deitybody

## Daily (30–40 min)

1. **Seat** 2 min
2. **Phonetic embodiment** 8 min — speak slowly; feel tongue/velum/throat/breath/folds
3. **VBT axis** 5 min — heart → saḥ → out · haṃ → heart (comfortable breath)
4. **Mātṛkā nyāsa** 15 min — glyph + sound + locus (touch lightly)
5. **Internal nyāsa** 5 min — no touching
6. **Silence / log** 2 min
