# mxth engines — vendored from prx0r/mxth (MathArtist archives)

- `chladni.js` + `cymatics.js` — Chladni plate renderer + Bessel/square modes.
  cymatics.js derives from evoluteur/cymatics (MIT, Olivier Giuliani 2026);
  equations retained, observation/render layers are MathArtist's.
- `tiny_maps.js` — compact mathematical maps (polar/radial warps etc.) from
  user-supplied 2026-09-17 Processing sketches. Unwired here (queued).
