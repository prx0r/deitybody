## Eight anahata-concentrations (practice ladder, ~19941-20124)

1. Petaline (kaṅ→ṭhaṅ japa → circular deep-red wheel) · 2. Mahābhūta (hexagon→Yaṃ) ·
3. Tanmātra (Yaṃ only, touch) · 4. Vāyu (deity on antelope appears) ·
5. Isha (from bindu; white; levitation-grade pranayama) · 6. Kakini (yellow/red/white uses) ·
7. Bana-liṅga (triangle→liṅga→bindu) · 8. Jīvatman (steady flame, I dissolved).

## Breath forms

- Fourth Form: suspend in anahata on air + Yaṃ + Isha (~7228-29).
- Bhutashuddhi 16-64-32: inhale left Yaṃ / hold / exhale right; then Raṃ red burning (~12244-99, 26333-45).
- Apāna-up + praṇa-mantra Yaṃ conducts Kundalini+jīva from anahata to sahasrāra (~11666-70).

## False friends flagged

- 16-white "anahata" = indu/moon-region, unrelated (~21649-58).
- 16-white heart lotus (Tararahasya/Brahmananda) = special concentration, not the standard 12.
- Kantha-chakra misnamed anahata in Narayana system (~14234-41).
- Kakini↔Lakini↔Bhuwaneshwari forms are purpose-specific, not interchangeable.
