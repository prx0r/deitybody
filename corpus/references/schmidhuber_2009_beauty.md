# Schmidhuber 2009 — notes for memory engine (not practice OS)

**Paper:** Jürgen Schmidhuber, *Simple Algorithmic Theory of Subjective Beauty, Novelty, Surprise, Interestingness, Attention, Curiosity, Creativity, Art, Science, Music, Jokes*, SICE J. 48(1) 2009.
**DOI:** 10.11499/sicejl.48.21
**PDF:** https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/sicejl/48/1/48_21/_pdf/-char/en

## Claims worth using

- Data is subjectively **beautiful** when a limited observer **compresses/predicts it better**.
- **Curiosity** seeks non-arbitrary regularity that enables further compression progress.
- **Interestingness** ≈ first derivative of compressibility (learning-curve steepness).
